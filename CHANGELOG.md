# Changelog

## 1.0.0
Initial release.

- Camera viewfinder (`O`) with date-stamped photos that leave out the HUD and menus.
- Ask nearby villagers, children and pets to pose for a photo. How villagers answer depends on your friendship.
- Automatic milestone photos: day one, new year, engagement, wedding, children, pets, house upgrades, best friends, greenhouse, Community Center or Joja ceremony, perfection.
- Album menu (`L`) with polaroid pages, and filters for milestones, favorites, posed photos, years and people.
- Photo viewer with captions, favorites, delete, and polaroid export.
- "On this day" morning reminders.
- Generic Mod Config Menu support, controller support, and console commands.
